Design

As a Chassis Engineer, I designed many safety critical components of our current car. This included changing the roll cage and foot box dimensions for taller drivers and running FEA to make sure these changes still resulted in a safe chassis. I also designed the firewall, splash guard, and various tabs making sure that it was easy to fabricate in house and could be easily disassembled to reach other parts of the car.

Fabrication

For fabrication season, I worked to create a wooden jig so that we could tack the tubes in place for welding. I also used the lathe and mill to create components for other sub-teams. Using OnShape, I also designed 3d printable jigs for welding. We took into account what angle the tabs and tubes connected at and then included space for tabs to get tacked while still being supported.

Leadership

As a chassis lead, I worked with other team leads on planning the fabrication cycle, shortening from 2 semesters to 1 semester. I also made freshman orientation tasks, helping new members learn how to design, CAD, and go through a design review.
