This summer, I worked as a mechanical engineering intern at change:Water Labs.

I was able to:

  • redesign a compact, water resistant, toilet cover/seat actuation mechanism, replacing a linear actuator ramp system with a servo-driven design to address insufficient force and friction losses.
  • develop Arduino code to allow for sensor based actuation of toilet cover and to automate fatigue testing, evaluating the servo for over 100 cycles.
  • integrate an off the shelf push-lock mechanism to hold a toilet cover in place, reducing reliance on continuous servo actuation.
  • and develop then evaporation test an origami inspired membrane cover used to protect the membrane against damage.
